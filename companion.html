OP-1

Install plugins and sounds, and manage what's on your OP-1.

⚠︎
This browser can't reach the OP-1 over USB.Sending songs and installing plugins needs Web MIDI, which Safari (including every browser on iPhone and iPad) doesn't have. Use Chrome or Edge on a Mac or PC for those. Everything else here works.

Install on OP-1

Any op1r plugin: Jazz Band, its sound packs, synths, effects, sequencers, tools.

Connect the OP-1 with USB in normal mode. After the upload, press T1 on the OP-1 to install and restart (T2 cancels).

  1. Plugins with the same name replace the one on the OP-1.
  2. Jazz Band's sound packs (jbpiano, jbbass, jbguitar, jbdrums, and jbnylon for João Gilberto) are in the sound packs folder. Every player uses its pack (Jazz Band 1.5; install the new jbdrums for toms, shaker and conga).
  3. Needs an op1r base with the plugin loader (update protocol 3+). For the base itself use the op1r installer.

Plugins on your OP-1

All plugins share 4 MB. Ones that don't fit are skipped at startup.

Connect the OP-1 with USB in normal mode.

The largest synths and effects are pre-selected when space is short. Removed op1r plugins can be reinstalled from your op1r build.

Your own instrument samples (disk mode, slots 6–8)

Drop recordings of single notes (WAV, AIFF, MP3), ideally one every 4–6 semitones. Pitch is detected; fix it if needed. Up to 16 notes per instrument, 6 seconds in total. Sound packs above take priority over these.
